IAM Lens: six logo concepts

Six original marks for a two-way GCP IAM lookup. Each is built on a 128 grid with the artwork inside the central 96, shown on light and dark, at real toolbar sizes, and as a 4x pixel preview. Colour comes from the product: console blue for the mark, read / write / admin hues only where the idea needs them.

01 Keyhole lens02 Permission anatomy03 Prism04 Shield lens05 Two-way link06 Viewfinder IL
01

Keyhole lens

A solid lens disc with a keyhole cut out of it: the thing you look through is also the thing that grants access. One shape, one cut, one handle.

Strengths
Strongest silhouette at 16 px. Instantly says "lookup" and "access".
Risks
Closest cousin to the old lens-and-key icon; magnifier plus keyhole is a familiar security trope.

02

Permission anatomy

The product's signature token, service.resource.verb, drawn as three segments. The verb is lit in the privilege hue and underlined with the anatomy bracket.

Strengths
Unmistakably ours; ties directly to the in-app token and to the risk palette.
Risks
Wide, low shape leaves much of a square slot empty; reads as "a bar" until you know the token.

03

Prism

A role is a prism: one beam goes in, and it fans out into read, write and admin. The only mark that carries the full risk palette as its idea.

Strengths
Colourful, memorable, explains what a role does. Distinct from every other concept.
Risks
Prism plus rainbow beam resembles well-known album-art imagery; three thin rays are fragile at 16 px.

04

Shield lens

A shield whose negative space is a magnifier: least privilege, inspected. The lens is carved out, so it takes the background colour on any surface.

Strengths
Clear security meaning, compact and bold, works as a tile-less mark.
Risks
Shields are the most common security icon; differentiation rests on the cut-out only.

05

Two-way link

A filled role node and a ring permission node joined by an L-shaped path with arrowheads at both ends: the role to permission lookup runs both ways.

Strengths
Describes the two-way feature directly; the L hints at the Lens initial.
Risks
Most diagram-like, so least "logo"; the arrowheads vanish at 16 px and leave nodes plus an elbow.

06

Viewfinder IL

Four camera-style corner brackets frame an I and an L cut from straight bars: inspecting IAM, with the initials as the subject.

Strengths
Typographic and ownable; calm geometry; no security cliche.
Risks
Needs the label to be understood; the glyphs are small inside the frame at 16 px.
